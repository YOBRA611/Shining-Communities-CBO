Who We Are

Giving Communities a Voice for Inclusive Development

Shining Communities CBO is Kenya’s advocacy initiative for development. Our work focuses on giving voice to communities with a view to reducing the marginalisation gap in the areas of gender, children and youth, water and sanitation rights, and global trade.

We seek to generate knowledge around these areas, create awareness among relevant actors, and build a rationale for meaningful action. Our approach involves reviewing current trends, identifying gaps and challenges, understanding what needs to be done, and identifying platforms through which community voices can be heard.

The information and experiences gathered are developed into papers, newsletters, posters, and publications. These are shared through seminars, workshops, international meetings, conferences, and digital platforms, including our website.

Our Purpose

Creating Space for Every Community Voice

Shining Communities CBO exists to give communities a voice and contribute towards reducing the marginalization gap. Through knowledge generation, awareness creation and meaningful engagement, we work to ensure that communities are better positioned to participate in issues that affect their lives and development.

Giving voice to communities with a view to reducing the marginalization gap.

Our Thrust

Giving Communities a Voice

Creating opportunities for communities to express their experiences, concerns, priorities and aspirations on matters affecting their development.

Reducing Marginalization

Addressing gaps that prevent individuals and communities from being meaningfully included in social, economic and development processes.

Creating Awareness

Generating and sharing knowledge that helps communities, institutions and other actors understand important issues and emerging challenges.

Promoting Community Participation

Encouraging active participation in discussions, initiatives, platforms and decisions that shape community wellbeing and development.

Turning Knowledge into Action

Transforming information, research and community experiences into practical advocacy, awareness initiatives and meaningful action.
